## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an editorial, feminine, and sophisticated design narrative tailored for high-end graphic design and digital marketing portfolios. The visual tone balances classic editorial prestige with contemporary warmth, welcoming clients with poise, creative flair, and clarity. 

The aesthetic is rooted in warm editorial minimalism combined with soft tactile elegance. It avoids cold clinical layouts in favor of inviting, spacious compositions with generous breathing room, high-contrast serif headlines, tactile pill-shaped elements, and ultra-soft rose-tinted illumination. Every touchpoint should evoke a sense of crafted luxury, intentional care, and delicate structure.

## Colors

The palette is built around soft rose hues grounded by deep, dignified mauve tones to maintain high legibility and contrast.

- **Background Canvas:** Light cream white (`#FFF8F9`) creates a soft, warm environment that avoids the stark sterility of pure digital white.
- **Surface & Containers:** Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) layered above the cream ground, framed with delicate blush borders (`#F3CCD3` and `#FADADD`).
- **Primary & Secondary Accents:** Rose pink (`#F4A6B8`) serves as the hero focal hue, supported by dusty rose (`#D98BA0`) for interactive states, focused accents, and elevated borders.
- **Tertiary & Highlights:** Soft rose sparkle (`#EBB0BE`) and faint blush glow (`#FDECEF`) add subtle dimension and delicate hover feedback.
- **Typography & Content:** Headlines utilize deep mauve (`#6B3A4A`) for striking editorial contrast, while body copy flows in soft warm muted mauve (`#8C5E6D`). Muted labels and secondary metadata use subtle dusty mauve (`#A57B8A`).

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial atmosphere through intentional typographic pairing:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** High-contrast transitional serifs provide grace, dramatic cadence, and editorial credibility. Italic variations are encouraged for pull quotes, accent keywords, and case study subtitles.
- **Body & Controls (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern geometric sans with soft, friendly terminals that ensure effortless readability across editorial essays, portfolio briefs, and interactive components.
- **Scale Continuity:** Large display and headline sizes transition to designated `-mobile` scales beneath tablet breakpoints to prevent awkward wrap breaks while maintaining striking hierarchy.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure relies on a fluid 12-column grid system paired with dynamic masonry arrangements for design galleries and marketing artifacts.

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12 columns with a 1.5rem gutter and 3rem outer margins. Content containers max out at 1280px to retain intimate editorial focus.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8 columns with a 1.25rem gutter and 2rem outer margins.
- **Mobile (Below 768px):** 4 columns with a 1rem gutter and 1.25rem outer margins. Multi-column showcase cards compress into a stacked single-column flow with offset visual padding.
- **Rhythm:** Vertical spacing favors airy proportions (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between narrative portfolio sections, preserving the open feel of printed art monographs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids heavy, neutral drop shadows in favor of luminous, tinted atmospheric diffusion:

- **Surface Tiers:** Background levels sit on `#FFF8F9`. Primary content cards and editorial sheets lift slightly via `#FFFFFF` surfaces bounded by crisp 1px borders in `#FADADD`.
- **Rose Diffusion Shadows:** Floating elements and interactive cards implement an ultra-soft rose shadow (`0 8px 30px -4px rgba(217, 139, 160, 0.12)`).
- **Hover Elevation:** On hover, elevated containers transition to `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(217, 139, 160, 0.20)`, accompanied by a subtle 1px border shift to `#F3CCD3`.
- **Frosted Overlays:** Modal and navigation backdrops apply a frosted cream blur (`rgba(255, 248, 249, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`).

## Shapes

The design system embraces soft, pill-shaped geometry that complements the curved nuances of Plus Jakarta Sans:

- **Buttons & Chips:** Full pill curves (`9999px` or level `3` standard token `1rem` on compact elements) providing approachable, touch-friendly affordances.
- **Content Cards & Containers:** Generously sculpted with `1rem` (`16px`) to `1.5rem` (`24px`) radii, creating smooth visual envelopes for imagery and text.
- **Images & Case Study Previews:** Softly rounded with `1rem` outer corners to nestle organically into layout columns.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary:* Filled with `#F4A6B8`, text in `#6B3A4A` (bold), full pill radius, soft rose glow shadow. Transitions on hover to `#D98BA0` with crisp white text.
  - *Secondary / Ghost:* Outlined with a 1.5px `#F3CCD3` stroke, cream/white fill, text in `#6B3A4A`. Shifts to `#FDECEF` fill on hover.
- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Pill-shaped tags with `0.25rem` vertical and `0.75rem` horizontal padding. Faint `#FDECEF` background with `#8C5E6D` text in `label-sm` tracking.
- **Cards & Case Study Tiles:**
  - White surface, `1px solid #FADADD`, rounded at `1.25rem`. Houses high-res mockups with seamless padding (`space-md`). Hover triggers a delicate 2px vertical lift and rose glow elevation.
- **Input Fields:**
  - Pill or soft-rounded (`1rem`) white inputs with `1px solid #FADADD` borders. Deep mauve placeholder text. On focus, transitions to a 1.5px `#D98BA0` border with a subtle 3px outer halo in `rgba(244, 166, 184, 0.25)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:**
  - Rounded-sm for checkboxes, fully circular for radios. Borders in `#D98BA0`, filling with `#F4A6B8` when selected, paired with a white checkmark or inner dot.
- **Lists & Editorial Accents:**
  - Custom list bullets utilize miniature rose diamond accents or subtle sparkle glyphs (`✦`) styled in `#D98BA0`.
- **Interactive Badges:**
  - Floating pill markers for project tags (e.g., "Brand Identity", "Marketing Strategy") with soft semi-translucent blush backings.
